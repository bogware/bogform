# Bogform Internals

This section covers the internals of Bogform and explains how
plans are generated, the lifecycle of a provider, etc. The goal
of this section is to remove any notion of "magic" from Bogform.
We want you to be able to trust and understand what Bogform is
doing to function.

:::note
Knowledge of Bogform internals is not
required to use Bogform. If you aren't interested in the internals
of Bogform, you may safely skip this section.
:::
